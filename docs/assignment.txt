Problem Statement
A large energy operator runs field jobs (workovers, maintenance, remediation). Each job is planned and safety-reviewed up front — approved scope, permits, hazards, controls. Call this the planned scope.
In the field, crews routinely identify additional work once they've mobilized — a degraded valve to swap, a stuck tool to recover. Call this emergent scope. This added work hasn't been through the same planning, permitting, or hazard review as the planned scope, so new hazards get introduced into the same job without formal visibility.
Early signals of emergent scope show up in the job's chat channel (assume Microsoft Teams) — free-text, informal, near real time, mixed with operational chatter and noise.
Goal: design an AI system that detects emerging risk from these signals early enough for humans to intervene. Stop-work authority stays with people.
Assumptions
A job package per job (work plan, permits, JSA, controls) available at or before mobilization.
Enterprise safety standards and historical learnings, shared across jobs.
One chat channel per job, free-text, near real time.
Scale: roughly 10²–10³ concurrent jobs.
